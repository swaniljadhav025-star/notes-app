📒 Notes

Text notes and money notes with automatic balance. Your data is backed up to your own private GitHub repo and to your phone storage.

⬇ Download APK

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download APK and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
  3. Tap Install, then open Notes.

Update the app

  1. Download the APK again and install it over the old one. Your data stays.
